# Task: why these three ovens differ

Write a markdown section a fabricator can use. Cover, for each oven and then as a comparison:

1. Architecture and subsystems (shell, core, insulation, floor, burner, air, flue, door, dock, controls).
2. Why the mouth is the full chamber width on Arc Lite and Tread and only about 65% on Dome XL. Tie that to wood being possible on one oven and forbidden on the others.
3. Why stone thickness is 12 mm on the two light ovens and 30 mm on Dome XL. Estimate stone mass and stored energy (cordierite 2100 kg/m3, cp 950 J/kgK, delta 480 K) and compare with the published heat-up times and burner power. Show the capture-fraction implication. Do not pretend the estimate is a measurement.
4. Why one insulation layer vs three, in mass, heat loss, recovery between pizzas, and touch risk. Gozney publishes layer count, not thickness.
5. Why Tread is heavier than Arc Lite (13.5 vs 12.0 kg) even though both are one-layer 12 inch ovens. Use the material list: handles, dock, die-cast landing, thermometer, deeper floor.
6. Why the shells are pressings (SPCC or aluminised) plus a 304 core and a die-cast landing, rather than a one-piece casting or a fully fabricated sheet-metal dome.
7. Flame-failure device, clearances, door rules, and wind. What each rule is protecting.
8. A side-by-side table of published numbers, then a second table of inferred ratios.

Keep it dense. No marketing voice. End with five decisions a home builder should copy and five they should not copy because they exist to hit a price, a mass, or a certification, not because the physics requires them.
