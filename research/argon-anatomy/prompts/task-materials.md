# Task: materials a home builder can actually use

Gozney's published construction is pressed steel skins, a 304 inner core, alkaline-earth silicate fibre, cordierite, and die-cast aluminium landings. Adib asked for alternatives they did not use, judged for his shop.

Write markdown. For each material give: where it sits (inner vault, insulation, outer shell, floor), continuous temperature, food-contact verdict, Adelaide sourcing with an estimated AUD retail band and a second-hand band (label every price ESTIMATE), difficulty, and the failure mode.

Cover at least:
- Cast refractory (high-alumina castable) inner dome
- Perlite or vermiculite concrete (insulating, not structural food surface)
- Ceramic fibre blanket and AES (calcium-magnesium silicate) blanket, including the dust and binder rules
- Calcium silicate board
- Cordierite and firebrick floors, and why steel or cast iron is the wrong floor at 450–500 C (effusivity)
- Recycled gas bottles and kegs as an inner vault, with the safety rule that the vessel is cold-cut, valve removed, and never a pressure vessel again
- Spun or beaten aluminium as an OUTER cold shell only. State the temperature where it stops being structure
- Composite shells (GRP, cement board) and why they fail next to a flame
- Microporous board, as a bought slab, not a DIY mix

Then a full section on Adib's idea: a turned or coopered hardwood outer body that holds clay or castable plus insulation, so he does not make a metal outer cover.

Do the thermal arithmetic in the text:
- Target: inner gas 500 C, wood inner face at or below 80 C in steady state, ambient 20 C, still outdoor air, h_ext about 10 W/m2K, h_in about 25 W/m2K.
- Propose a layer stack (inner refractory thickness, fibre or perlite thickness, optional air gap, hardwood thickness 18–25 mm).
- Solve the 1D resistances and report heat flux, wood inner-face temperature, and wood outer-face temperature.
- Then say what breaks the model: flame roll out the mouth, a flue penetration, a hot landing, rain, and a wood arch at the opening.
- Mitigations that could make the barrel work: non-combustible mouth surround and flue collar in 304, ventilated cavity or a calcium-silicate break so wood never touches the fibre's hot face, species available in South Australia (sugar gum, red gum, blackwood, jarrah brought in), moisture movement vs thermal movement, char layer is not an acceptable design state.
- Verdict: viable or not as an outer cold shell, and the conditions. Be direct if the mouth and the flue must stay metal or refractory.

Flag certified-buy items in a short list at the end.
