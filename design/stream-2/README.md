# Stream 2 — modular

Morning and Moondancer stay the round, short baselines. This stream is a stack of three modules in the spirit of a Hot Ash cooker: a fire pit, a floating steel hearth, and a long hood. Gas only. The bought burner, valve, regulator, and hose are the same parts as the other ovens. No cordierite, no casting, no cut stone.

The floor is a 4 mm mild plate on three microporous pads. It is not bolted. In-plane growth is 1.7 mm and the side gap is 12 mm, so the plate cannot go into compression. Through-thickness bow at a 300°C soak is 0.002 mm. Self-weight sag on the long span is 0.27 mm. A 300°C steel face meets dough at 275°C. Cordierite at 450°C meets it at 289°C. The deck is run at 300°C on purpose.

The flame rises in the rear gutter, washes the soffit, and leaves by a front flue. The mouth is 0.63 of the front crown. Steak sits on a rack 50 mm under the soffit. At 550°C and a view factor of 0.65 the radiant flux is 13.8 kW/m². Bread uses the door and the damper. Wood is jarrah grips and feet, and a gidgee peel rest in front of the sill. The firebox walls are bare and run near 390°C. The handles stand off them. Shrykos is the one to build.

Masses are the analytical solid plus the 1.85 kg burner in the pit. Skin and underside are the CalculiX result, which matched the resistance sum. Costs are ESTIMATE, AUD, Adelaide, October 2026, bottle excluded.

Vermax was a tall empty box over a low soffit. Arrax cut the crown down to the soffit with a square step. Shrykos folds that step at 45°, a brake bend, so the ceiling descends over the pizza. The grate stays in the pit.
