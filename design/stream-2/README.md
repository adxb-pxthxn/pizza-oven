# Stream 2 — modular

Morning and Moondancer stay the round, short baselines. This stream is a stack in the spirit of a Hot Ash cooker: a fire pit, a floating steel hearth, a brow, and a tunnel. Gas only. The bought burner, valve, regulator, and hose are the same parts as the other ovens. No cordierite, no casting, no cut stone.

The floor is a 4 mm mild plate on three microporous pads. It is not bolted. In-plane growth is 1.7 mm and the side gap is 12 mm, so the plate cannot go into compression. Through-thickness bow at a 300°C soak is 0.002 mm. Self-weight sag on the long span is 0.27 mm. A 300°C steel face meets dough at 275°C. Cordierite at 450°C meets it at 289°C. The deck is run at 300°C on purpose.

The flame rises in the rear gutter, washes the soffit, and leaves by a front flue. The mouth is 0.63 of the front crown. At 550°C and a view factor of 0.65 the radiant flux is 13.8 kW/m². On Vhagar that surface is the plate over the steak, carried on 8 mm rods. The rack is 50 mm under it and travels with the plate. Bread uses the door, the damper, and a rope in the door gap. Wood is jarrah grips and feet, and a gidgee peel rest in front of the sill. The firebox walls are bare and run near 390°C. The handles stand off them. A mild plate rides under the rear overhang and stands on the rim when the hood is off. Vhagar is the one to build. Slide the broiler plate aft for the pizza.

Masses are the analytical solid plus the 1.85 kg burner in the pit. Skin and underside are the CalculiX result, which matched the resistance sum. Costs are ESTIMATE, AUD, Adelaide, October 2026, bottle excluded.

Vermax was a tall empty box over a low soffit. Arrax cut the crown to the soffit. Shrykos folded that step. Morghul splits the fold. Seasmoke seats the brow and lifts the grate. Caraxes raises the roof over the pizza. Meleys puts a bail on the sill. Syrax shuts the damper. Sheepstealer puts a rope in the door gap. Vermithor slides the plate forward over the steak. Silverwing keeps that slide and changes the flats for rods. Vhagar stores a wind-back on the pit.
