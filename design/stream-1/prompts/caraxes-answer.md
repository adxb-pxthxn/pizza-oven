The 11 mm lower hollow earns its cut, as that is where the fingers actually hook to take the ~10 kg load per hand and where a sharp corner would bite into the joints. The 8 by 4 mm thumb step on the top inner edge, however, is mostly carving for its own sake—and works against you here. With only a 20 mm standoff from a hot flat skin, inviting the thumb onto the top inner edge pushes it directly into the radiant heat zone while thinning out the top bulk that the palm and thumb web want to press against to stabilize a 38.7 kg long-vault body. Leave the top full to keep the palm bulk and naturally bias the thumb outward away from the skin, or simply break that top inner arris with a 3 mm chamfer so it doesn't dig in.
