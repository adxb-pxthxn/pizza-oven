The door is placed, not hinged. A 90 degree swing about the mouth jamb would cut the shell, which is why the earlier models set the door down in each pose.

The hinge pin is 8 mm stainless, vertical, at the port front corner, x = -338 mm, y = -91 mm, outboard of the skin. The door's port edge lies on that pin. Shut, the door is an arch over the mouth plus a wing out to the pin, and its bottom is 3 mm above the deck so it clears the sill. Open, the same door rotates 90 degrees and lies along the port side, outboard of the skin, from the pin back about 570 mm. It does not pass through the barrel. The pin is a clearance fit in the lugs, not a press fit.
