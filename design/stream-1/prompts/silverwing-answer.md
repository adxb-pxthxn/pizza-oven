That is the right hinge geometry if you want a single-axis swing that clears the barrel without a four-bar linkage, and a clearance-fit pin lets the door lift straight off when needed. However, extending the door panel itself into a full wing out to x = -338 mm makes the door too much part—it adds cantilevered weight, wastes sheet stock, and acts as an uninsulated fin that will warp under heat and pull the mouth seal out of flat. Instead, keep the door strictly sized to the mouth arch and bridge the span to the outboard pin with two narrow hinge straps (or a light rod frame). You get the exact same 90-degree stowage along the port skin with a fraction of the mass and thermal distortion.
