Sheepstealer is the next long-vault oven after Seasmoke. Same heat, same straps, same tray. Do not use tools. Reply in plain text.

Shop notes ask for three things. A handle that pivots down into a kick-stand, or into a peel rest and a door rest. An aluminium skin that ships as flat laser-cut blanks with roll lines and tabs, handles fixed to the frame. One short deadpan line, seen only when the oven is lifted. No fake thermometer. No timber on the skin.

The kick-stand is refused. The floor is a free 6 mm plate on six domed buttons, stops 4 mm clear. A 15 degree tilt puts the plate's weight along the deck. Oil on the buttons lets it walk onto the stops, and the six-point support is gone. The oven is not tilted hot. A cold tilt is not worth a leg.

The carry grip is also refused as the peel ledge. Grip centre is about (-277, 390, 335) mm. The peel ledge is about (0, -108, 53). The parked door is about (-345, 160, 156). The circle through those three points is centred inside the chamber, so one hinge on the cheek cannot reach them. A second link would exist to delete 0.11 kg of jarrah.

Sheepstealer keeps the heat. It adds a developed flat of the 1.0 mm skin: barrel blank on the mid-thickness arc, two half-disc ends, mouth and burner slot cut in the flats, 4.2 mm rivet holes on a 40 mm pitch, 12 mm spring-line tabs. It adds a 4 mm K-type gland in the stainless mouth frame, not through the fibre. The line "be vigilant. don't die." is scribed 0.3 mm into the underside of the base, not into the cooking steel.

Is the refusal right, and are the flat and the gland the parts worth making? A few sentences. No file writes.
