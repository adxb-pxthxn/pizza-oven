## A false sentence in Concept B

You wrote that the Ø430 × 20 stone lifts out through the 395 × 90 mouth by tilting 22°, and that the mouth diagonal is 403 mm. Check it. A disk of diameter D tilted so its projected width equals the mouth width W has projected height D·sin(acos(W/D)), and the 20 mm thickness adds more. For D=430, W=395, that tilt is 23.3° and the projected height is about 170 mm against a 90 mm mouth. The stone does not come out through that mouth. The brass cinch band, which lets the crown lift off, is the real access. Say so, and do not repeat the diagonal trick.

## What the coordinator will model

The three concepts agree on one thing: a thermally serious 15 inch circular oven does not sit on a 517 mm tailgate, and a wood shell that stays under 80°C is a two-person lift near 45 kg. Concept B is the only one-person oven. The wood in B is the handles, not the body. That is the hybrid that survives the requirements: jarrah where a hand closes on it, brass where the eye looks for a joint, stainless everywhere the heat is.

Attack this before you bless it:

1. Mouth 395 mm for a 381 mm pizza is 7 mm per side. Arc Lite gives a 305 mm pizza about 10 mm per side through a mouth as wide as the chamber. Is 420 mm wide and 95 mm high the right mouth on a 470 mm chamber, ratio 95/165 = 0.576, or does widening the mouth cost a flame roll you can quantify?
