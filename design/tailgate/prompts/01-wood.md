Develop ONE concept: Adib's ideal look, taken as far as the physics allows.

Ideal look: a turned wood outer body with metal accents. Machined or rolled rings, bands, handles, a mouth surround, a badge. The interior (liner, insulation, floor, burner bay) is whatever is technically best. The wood is thermally safe using the passing stack: 75 mm AES blanket + 50 mm calcium silicate, wood inner face near 74°C, or a better stack you calculate the same way. Handle wood movement, rain, finish, and heat cycling. The diameter is large for any lathe whose swing was not published, so the body is coopered or segmented and only optionally finish-turned.

This concept has to be a real tailgate oven, not a sculpture. Weigh the wood honestly. If the diameter will not sit on the Shark tailgate, give it legs or a stand and say what that does to the evening. Optimise mass: stave thickness, species, what is wood and what is a metal crown, whether the dome the eye sees is wood or a metal cap over a wood drum. A full hemisphere of 20 mm jarrah is probably the wrong answer. Show the alternative you rejected and the kilograms it cost.

Return, in this order:

1. A name, and one paragraph on what the object is.
2. A dimensioned description: plan diameter, body height, floor diameter and thickness, chamber inside diameter, apex height above the floor, mouth width and height, mouth/apex, wall stack from the gas outward with each thickness, leg and handle geometry, stowed envelope.
3. Mass table. Each line is part, material, volume in litres or a shown formula, density, kilograms. A total. A one-person carry verdict.
4. Heat-up. Stone mass, a rough time to a 400°C floor at your burner power, and the shell temperatures (wood inner, wood outer, handle).
5. How it is made, part by part, naming the makerspace machine or "university workshop" or "bought".
6. Weather, vibration, pack-down, cleaning.
7. Wood movement detail: stave count, groove, hoop, finish, what is allowed to move.
8. Cost band, AUD, new vs second-hand vs workshop-made. Certified gas parts listed separately and never given a homemade price.
9. The three worst things about this concept. Do not soften them.
10. A one-screen parts list another engineer could model: named solids, in millimetres.

Do not propose a second concept. Do not talk yourself into abandoning the wood body. Make this one as good as it can be, and be brutal about the kilograms.
